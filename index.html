ACOUSTICS SCIENCE & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do Brown, Pink, White, and Grey noise differ?

Brown noise has power density inversely proportional to frequency squared (1/f²), producing deep, waterfall-like rumble. Pink noise decreases by 3dB per octave (1/f), mimicking natural rain and rustling wind. White noise distributes energy evenly across all frequencies (flat 0dB/octave), while Grey noise is filtered through an inverted psychoacoustic equal-loudness curve so every frequency sounds equally loud to human ears.

What is "Speech Blocker" and how does it mask chatter?

Human speech intelligibility lives primarily within the 250Hz – 3.8kHz frequency band (formants and sibilants). Speech Blocker shapes acoustic energy into this critical range with conversational roll-offs, raising your Speech Reception Threshold so nearby speech becomes an unintelligible blur without causing auditory fatigue.

How do I customize the 10 frequency sliders?

Hover over the frequency curve wave on the main screen to reveal the 10-band equalizer sliders. Drag any band up (to +24dB) or down (to -24dB). An integrated studio DynamicsCompressor safety limiter ensures boosts never produce digital distortion or clipping.

What are Infra, Ultra, and Ear Massage?

Infra cuts off frequencies above 60Hz for deep seismic rumble. Ultra isolates frequencies above 8kHz for light air. Ear Massage uses dual out-of-phase low-frequency oscillators (LFOs) to sweep filters across both ears for a soothing binaural wave.
