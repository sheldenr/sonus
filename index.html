Frequently Asked Questions

Acoustics, privacy & science
How do the different noise colors work?

Brown noise has power density inversely proportional to frequency squared (1/f²), yielding deep, heavy waterfall-like rumbles that relax the nervous system. Pink noise decreases by 3dB per octave (1/f), mimicking natural rain and rustling wind. White noise distributes energy evenly across all frequencies (flat 0dB/octave), while Grey noise is filtered through an inverted psychoacoustic equal-loudness curve so every frequency sounds equally loud to human ears.

What is "Speech Blocker" and how does it protect focus?

Human speech intelligibility resides primarily within the 250Hz – 3.8kHz frequency band (vocal formants and consonant bursts). Speech Blocker concentrates acoustic energy into this critical range with conversational roll-offs, effectively raising your Speech Reception Threshold so nearby conversation becomes unintelligible background murmur without causing auditory fatigue.

How do I use the 10-Band Equalizer to create custom noise?

Each vertical fader governs an octave band (31Hz up to 16kHz). You can selectively boost any range up to +24dB or cut it down to -24dB. The audio pipeline features an integrated studio-grade limiter (DynamicsCompressorNode) so you can push and sculpt frequencies as hard as you like without digital clipping or distortion.

What are the "Infra", "Ultra", and "Ear Massage" presets?

Infra cuts off everything above 60Hz to produce a subterranean, seismic body rumble. Ultra isolates frequencies above 8kHz for light, airy hiss. Ear Massage uses dual out-of-phase low-frequency oscillators (LFOs) to slowly sweep resonant filters across both ears, creating a gentle binaural acoustic wave.

Is this application private and offline-capable?

Yes, 100%. All sound synthesis is calculated client-side in real time using math and the Web Audio API. No audio files are downloaded, no tracking scripts are executed, and no user data is sent anywhere. It runs seamlessly even without an internet connection.
