About the Developer

The modern web is perpetually loud. Notifications, intrusive trackers, and cognitive clutter fight constantly for your attention. I built Sonus to be the exact opposite: an uncompromised, beautiful sanctuary where you can sit down, put on headphones, and disappear into your work.

Every frequency in this generator runs directly in your browser through the native Web Audio API — there are no servers streaming audio files, no subscriptions, and zero analytics tracking your focus. Whether you use deep Brown noise to soothe an ADHD mind, Speech Blocker to mute open-office chatter, or sculpt your own custom 10-band profile, I hope this tool brings quiet clarity to your craft.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between White, Pink, and Brown noise?

White noise has equal spectral power across all frequencies (similar to crisp TV static). Pink noise drops 3 dB per octave, mimicking natural acoustic textures like rain or waterfalls. Brown noise drops 6 dB per octave, delivering a deep, warm low-end rumble like rolling ocean surf.

How does ambient noise help with ADHD and concentration?

Through a neurological phenomenon known as stochastic resonance, moderate non-intrusive sound adds optimal auditory stimulation to the nervous system. This raises focus signals above internal distraction thresholds, helping people with ADHD or racing thoughts enter deep flow states.

Can Sonus help mask tinnitus or hyperacusis?

Yes. Acoustic sound enrichment is a clinical mainstay for tinnitus management. By adjusting the 10-band equalizer to envelop or soften the pitch of your ringing, you reduce the contrast between the phantom sound and quiet environments, making it easier for the auditory cortex to ignore it.

Is any audio streamed or user data collected?

None whatsoever. Every frequency wave is calculated procedurally on-the-fly directly inside your browser using the native Web Audio API. There are no audio streams, zero tracking cookies, and it functions completely offline without internet access.

Which preset works best for blocking speech and office chatter?

The Speech Blocker preset is engineered to gently accentuate the 250 Hz to 4 kHz range where human consonants and intelligibility live. Blending this profile with Brown or Pink noise obscures spoken words into unintelligible ambient texture.
